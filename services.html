A CLOSER PERSPECTIVE

A connected engagement does not need a single large scope

Different stages can use different services. An architecture review may reveal a small integration requirement; a migration may expose an operational ownership gap. Keep the next step tied to the evidence from the previous one.

  1. Identify the immediate decision

    Be clear whether the organisation needs to choose a direction, specify a change, implement it or operate it.

  2. Choose the relevant owner

    Include the person accountable for the process or platform affected by the work.

  3. Agree the next deliverable

    Set a reviewable output for the engagement instead of committing to an indefinite transformation programme.

A USEFUL STARTING QUESTION

Can several services be combined?

Yes, where the work crosses disciplines. The engagement should still distinguish responsibilities, dependencies and acceptance criteria rather than merge them into a vague promise of end-to-end delivery.
