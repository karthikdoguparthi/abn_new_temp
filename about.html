ABOUT ACTIVE BRAINS

Practical thinking. Technical Expertise

ACTIVE BRAINS brings business analysis, architecture and engineering into the same conversation. Our focus is on making technology decisions understandable and the resulting systems useful to the people who depend on them.

EXPLORE THE FOCUS

What guides the work

Our practice spans business applications, enterprise data, cloud platforms and AI-assisted workflows. The common thread is a clear connection between the business problem and the work proposed to address it.

Understand before prescribing

A platform recommendation should follow an understanding of the process, the existing estate and the people involved. We examine those relationships before treating a technology as the answer.

Explain the important choices

Architecture has consequences for cost, support and future change. We make assumptions and trade-offs visible so the organisation can decide which consequences it is prepared to accept.

Enterprise Architecture ↗

Leave usable knowledge behind

Delivery should improve the client's ability to operate and change the result. Documentation, clear ownership and practical handover belong within the work rather than after it.

A CLOSER PERSPECTIVE

A working relationship built on clarity

Good collaboration depends on an agreed problem, access to the people who understand it and a realistic view of constraints. We aim to keep those three things visible as a project moves from discussion into implementation.

  1. Hear the operational story

    The people handling exceptions and workarounds often reveal requirements that do not appear in a high-level brief.

  2. Keep decisions reviewable

    A proposed change should explain its purpose, alternatives and the evidence used to judge it.

  3. Respect the next owner

    The team inheriting an application needs understandable configuration, recovery procedures and a record of significant decisions.

A USEFUL STARTING QUESTION

Where should a first conversation begin?

Describe the difficulty your organisation is trying to resolve and which people or systems it affects. You do not need to arrive with a preferred product or a completed technical specification.
