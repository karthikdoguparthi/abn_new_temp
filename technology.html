A CLOSER PERSPECTIVE

Ask questions that survive a product demonstration

A demonstration shows a selected path. Architecture work examines your users, information and operating conditions, including the cases a demonstration often leaves out.

  1. What must connect?

    Identify existing identities, applications and records that the proposed platform would need to work with.

  2. Who will own it?

    Confirm the skills, access and support responsibility needed after the implementation team leaves.

  3. What must be verified?

    Check the proposed product configuration, regional availability and commercial assumptions before making a commitment.
