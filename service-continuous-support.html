SPECIALIST PRACTICE / Continuous Support & AMC

Keep maintenance from becoming an emergency.

For organisations with established applications, a maintenance agreement makes routine upkeep, corrective work and response responsibilities explicit.

THE PROBLEM IN CONTEXT

Separate incidents from planned improvement

A failed integration and a request for a new report need different treatment. Defining those categories prevents urgent faults from consuming the entire improvement budget and avoids treating every change as an emergency.

Maintenance schedule

Organise dependency updates, configuration reviews and recurring operational checks around business constraints.

Incident classification

Agree severity criteria and routing based on business impact rather than the volume of incoming messages.

Change allowance

Define how minor enhancements are requested, estimated and prioritised within the support arrangement.

WHAT THE WORK INVOLVES

Continuous Support & AMC in practice.

  1. Establish the supported baseline

    Record versions, environments and known limitations before accepting maintenance responsibility.

  2. Prepare restoration procedures

    Document the steps and access required to recover common failure scenarios.

  3. Review recurring faults

    Use incident patterns to select corrective work that reduces repeat disruption.

ILLUSTRATIVE SCENARIO · NOT A CLIENT CASE STUDY

An example: a recurring overnight failure

A scheduled import repeatedly fails on the same record format. Maintenance should address validation and exception handling, not simply rerun the job indefinitely.

BEFORE AN ENGAGEMENT

Questions about Continuous Support & AMC.

Discuss this scope ↗
Are response hours automatically round the clock?

No. Coverage windows, response targets and exclusions must be agreed in the service contract for the systems involved.

Does maintenance include major new functionality?

Not by default. Substantial changes should be scoped separately so both parties understand delivery responsibility and cost.
