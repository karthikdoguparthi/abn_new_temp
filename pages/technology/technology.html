A CLOSER PERSPECTIVE

Questions to ask after the product demo

A demo shows the product at its best. Good architecture work looks at your users, your information and the way you operate, including the awkward cases a demo usually skips.

  1. What must it connect to?

    The existing user accounts, applications and records the new platform will need to work with.

  2. Who will own it?

    The skills, access and support responsibility you will need once the implementation team has gone.

  3. What needs checking?

    The exact product setup, availability in your region and the commercial assumptions, all confirmed before you commit.
