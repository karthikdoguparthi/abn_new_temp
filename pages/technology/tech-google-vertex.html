THE CHALLENGE

You can’t improve what you can’t repeat

It’s hard to judge a promising demo when its data, settings or success criteria keep shifting. We record every choice, so you can compare versions fairly and spot straight away when a change makes things worse.

Experiment records

A complete record of the data, settings and test examples behind every approach you try.

Application integration

The chosen AI capability connected to the business information it needs, returning results people can act on.

Release evaluation

Every new version checked against agreed examples before users see any change.

HOW WE DELIVER

From lab to live, with nothing left to chance.

  1. Define the output

    We agree whether the AI sorts, extracts, drafts or recommends, and exactly how its results will be used.

  2. Build a test set

    Realistic examples that show both what the AI does well and where it falls short.

  3. Name the owners

    Clear responsibility for approving changes, investigating odd results and maintaining the connections.

ILLUSTRATIVE SCENARIO · NOT A CLIENT CASE STUDY

An example: classifying incoming documents

Incoming documents are sorted automatically. When the AI isn’t confident, it sends the document to a person for review, instead of forcing it into a category it may have got wrong.

If the demo works, is it ready to go live?

Not yet. It also needs integration checks, a permissions review, operating procedures and evidence from realistic test cases. That’s exactly the gap we close.

Should we adopt every new model version straight away?

No. Test each change with your application first, because different behaviour can affect the people and processes that depend on it.
