PLATFORM PERSPECTIVE / Enterprise Knowledge & Search

The right answer, first time, with the source to prove it.

When knowledge is scattered across shared drives, intranets and applications, people waste time searching and still find the wrong version. We design enterprise search around the questions people really ask, the access rules you already have and the quality of your sources.

THE CHALLENGE

Found isn’t the same as right

A result can look relevant and still be out of date, or be something the person asking shouldn’t see. We make sure official content wins, access rules hold and retired documents stay retired.

Source inventory

A map of your useful content stores, who owns each one and how often it changes.

Retrieval design

Questions, document structure and tags working together to surface exactly the right passage or record.

Answer traceability

Every answer linked to its source, so people can check the original rather than trust a summary blindly.

HOW WE DELIVER

Built around real questions, tested on real content.

  1. Gather real questions

    We collect questions in your users’ own words, including some that should return no answer at all.

  2. Clean up the sources

    Duplicates, out-of-date documents and unowned content are dealt with before we add more.

  3. Test relevance and access

    We prove that useful results appear, and that restricted content stays hidden from people without permission.
