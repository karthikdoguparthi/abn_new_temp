THE CHALLENGE

Design for the bad day, not just the good one

Expired links, missing documents and confusing jargon are where portals lose people. A clear way to recover, and honest information about where a request stands, matter as much as the main transaction.

Journey prototypes

Early versions of the key screens, tested with real customer tasks before any production code is written.

Account and access design

Registration, sign-in, acting on someone’s behalf and record visibility, all tailored to your audience.

Service integration

Every request linked to the team and system that fulfils it, so nothing disappears without trace.

HOW WE DELIVER

Built around your customers, connected to your teams.

  1. Find the biggest frustration

    Support calls and workarounds tell us which customer journey to fix first for the quickest return.

  2. Test on every screen

    Forms, errors and uploads checked on phones, tablets and desktops, and with keyboard-only navigation.

  3. Close the loop

    Every request has an owner inside your organisation and a status your customer can understand.

ILLUSTRATIVE SCENARIO · NOT A CLIENT CASE STUDY

An example: a partner document portal

Partners upload the documents you need, see instantly what’s still outstanding and know exactly what to do next. Your reviewers work from the same record, so nobody chases attachments through inboxes.

YOUR QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Ready to give customers a better experience?

Discuss your portal ↗
Does the portal need every feature our staff use?

No. It should offer just the tasks your customers or partners need, with screens and permissions designed around them.

What happens if a connected system is down?

We decide in advance whether submissions are paused, queued or retried, and the portal tells customers honestly what’s happening instead of showing a false success.
