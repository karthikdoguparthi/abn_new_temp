A CLOSER PERSPECTIVE

One partner, many stages

Different stages call for different services. An architecture review may uncover a small integration job; a migration may reveal a process nobody owns. With Active Brains, each next step builds on what the last one taught us.

  1. Know what you’re deciding

    Choosing a direction, planning a change, building it or running it? The answer points straight to the right service.

  2. Involve the right owner

    Bring in the person accountable for the process or platform the work will affect.

  3. Commit to a clear result

    Agree a specific deliverable you can review, not an open-ended transformation programme.

A USEFUL STARTING QUESTION

Can several services be combined?

Yes. Many projects span more than one discipline, and one partner across all of them saves time and handovers. We still keep responsibilities, dependencies and sign-off criteria clear for each part, rather than hiding them inside a vague promise of “end-to-end delivery”.
