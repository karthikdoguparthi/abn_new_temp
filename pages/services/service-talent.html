THE CHALLENGE

Productive from week one

An extra pair of hands can’t fix an unclear backlog or a missing decision-maker. So before anyone starts, we agree the work, the contribution you expect and the people they’ll work with. That way they add value from the first week.

Role definition

The gap in your team turned into a clear role: responsibilities, experience required and what success looks like.

Onboarding checklist

Access, systems, documents and contacts ready before day one, so no time is wasted waiting.

Continuity plan

Decisions and work in progress recorded as they happen, so the knowledge stays with your team.

HOW WE DELIVER

Your team, temporarily stronger.

  1. Pin down the need

    Architecture, development, testing, analysis or operations: we confirm exactly what you need before proposing anyone.

  2. Join your rhythm

    Your specialist works within your planning and review routines, reporting to a named lead.

  3. Review on results

    We judge the assignment on what’s been delivered and how your needs have changed, not just on hours logged.

ILLUSTRATIVE SCENARIO · NOT A CLIENT CASE STUDY

An example: a migration workstream gap

A project needs a data-migration expert to map data, run practice migrations and check the results. Your permanent team keeps ownership of the platform and the final say on releases, and picks up the know-how along the way.

YOUR QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Need specialist skills on your project?

Discuss your team’s needs ↗
Who manages the specialist day to day?

You do, through a reporting line we agree at the start. Our specialists strengthen the workstreams you already lead; they don’t replace your management.

How do we keep the knowledge once they leave?

Handover happens throughout the assignment, not just in the final week, through shared files, review sessions and decisions written down as they’re made.
