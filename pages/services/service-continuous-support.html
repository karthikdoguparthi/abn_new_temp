SPECIALIST PRACTICE / Continuous Support & AMC

Support that stops problems coming back.

An annual maintenance contract (AMC) with Active Brains does more than answer the phone. It keeps your applications healthy with planned upkeep, fixes urgent faults in order of business impact and tackles root causes, so the same issue doesn’t spoil another Monday morning.

THE CHALLENGE

Not every request is an emergency

A broken integration and a request for a new report need very different responses. Treating them the same drains your budget and wears out your team. We separate urgent fixes from planned improvements, so both get the attention they deserve.

Maintenance schedule

Updates, configuration reviews and health checks, planned around your busiest periods rather than in the middle of them.

Incident classification

Priorities set by the impact on your business, not by who shouts loudest or how many emails arrive.

Change allowance

A simple, predictable way to request, estimate and schedule small improvements within your contract.

HOW WE DELIVER

Fewer incidents. Faster recovery.

  1. Agree the starting point

    Before we take over, we record versions, environments and known issues, so everyone knows exactly what is covered.

  2. Prepare for the worst

    Step-by-step recovery procedures for the most likely failures, ready before they’re needed.

  3. Fix causes, not symptoms

    We track recurring faults and remove them at the source, so support gets quieter over time.

ILLUSTRATIVE SCENARIO · NOT A CLIENT CASE STUDY

An example: a recurring overnight failure

An overnight data import fails on the same type of record every few weeks. Rather than rerunning it each morning, we fix how that record is checked and handled, and the alerts stop.

YOUR QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Looking for dependable support?

Ask about an AMC ↗
Is support provided 24 hours a day?

Only if you need it. Support hours, response targets and exclusions are agreed up front for each system, so you pay for cover that matches your business.

Does maintenance cover major new features?

Not by default. Larger changes are scoped and priced separately, so there are no surprises on either side.
