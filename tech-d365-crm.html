PLATFORM PERSPECTIVE / Dynamics 365 Sales & Customer Service

Make customer information useful at the point of contact.

For sales and service teams, a CRM implementation should clarify the next action, preserve relevant customer context and reduce ambiguity about who owns the relationship.

THE PROBLEM IN CONTEXT

Configure around decisions, not fields

Adding more fields can create a fuller record without helping anyone act. We examine what a seller or service colleague needs to decide, then design the record and workflow around that moment.

Customer record design

Define account, contact and relationship information with clear ownership and duplicate-handling rules.

Sales progression

Agree the evidence required to move opportunities between stages and the actions each stage should prompt.

Service case journeys

Map categorisation, routing, escalation and resolution so support records reflect actual work.

WHAT THE WORK INVOLVES

Dynamics 365 Sales & Customer Service
in practice.

  1. Observe customer handoffs

    Find where information is lost between marketing, sales, delivery and support.

  2. Define useful record requirements

    Make data collection proportional to the decisions and reporting it supports.

  3. Test with frontline users

    Validate the experience using realistic calls, opportunities and service cases.

BEFORE AN ENGAGEMENT

Questions about Dynamics 365 Sales & Customer Service.

Discuss this scope ↗
Can we migrate duplicate customer records unchanged?

Technically possible does not mean advisable. Matching and ownership rules should be agreed so the new platform does not inherit avoidable confusion.

How should adoption be assessed?

Look beyond logins. Review whether users maintain meaningful records and can complete the intended customer tasks.
