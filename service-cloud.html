THE PROBLEM IN CONTEXT

Migration is an operational change

A successful transfer of files and services does not prove that the workload is ready. Teams also need to know how to restore it, who responds to incidents and what happens when connections to remaining systems fail.

Workload assessment

Document dependencies, access patterns and constraints that influence migration suitability and sequencing.

Landing-zone design

Establish account structure, access boundaries, network decisions and resource ownership before workloads multiply.

Cutover planning

Define rehearsal steps, validation checks, rollback conditions and communications for the migration window.

BEFORE AN ENGAGEMENT

Questions about Cloud Services (UK & EU).

Discuss this scope ↗
Can hosting location alone establish compliance?

No. Location is one input alongside access, contracts, processing activities and operational controls. Requirements need to be assessed for the specific workload.

Do all workloads benefit from immediate migration?

No. Some should be retained or modernised first. The assessment should explain why a move is appropriate and what would make it premature.
